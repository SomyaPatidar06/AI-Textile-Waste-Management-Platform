## 3. Disclosed Limitations & Technical Assumptions

For transparency in the public GitHub release, the following technical limitations are formally disclosed in the documentation:

1. **Option B Optical Policy:** The platform's optical classification models estimate fiber type from surface RGB imagery. They do not constitute chemical fiber dissolution analysis or laboratory-certified fiber composition.
2. **Material Classifier Taxonomy:** The primary neural model `fabric_model_v2.pth` is trained on **6 natural textile fibers** (`Abaca`, `Cotton`, `Hessian`, `Linen`, `Silk`, `Wool`). Synthetic fibers and blended consignments are handled through inventory sorting and circularity pathways, but optical classification is specialized for natural fibers.
3. **Spatial Bounding Boxes:** Spatial defect boxes generated by the multi-scale sliding-window detector serve as **coarse visual guidance regions** for human operators, not dense sub-millimeter segmentation boundaries.
4. **Lighting Sensitivity:** As proven in perturbation testing, image acquisition requires controlled diffuse illumination; severe specular glare degrades weave classification accuracy.
